Cancellation & Refund Policy

Order status and payment status are separate. An order can be cancelled while its refund is still being processed. We only show “refunded” once our payment provider has confirmed it.

1. What you can do at each stage

Stage of your order What happens
Before payment You can cancel freely. Nothing is charged.
Payment pending or unclear We check the result with the payment provider first. If you were charged and the order cannot go ahead, you are refunded in full.
Paid, store has not accepted yet You can cancel and receive a full refund.
Store accepted, not yet preparing You can cancel and receive a full refund.
Being prepared or ready Contact support. Because the food is already made, we decide each case. A refund is not guaranteed and may be partial.
Driver has collected or is on the way Contact support. We look at the food, the driver’s time and your refund together.
Delivered You cannot cancel. Report a problem instead (see section 3).

2. When the store or Skraal cancels

If a store rejects your order, does not respond within 5 minutes, or cannot continue, you receive a full refund. The same applies if we cannot find a driver for a delivery order and have to cancel it.

3. Problems with a delivered order

Report it in the app within 24 hours of delivery. Examples: missing or wrong items, food quality or safety, order not delivered, wrong address caused by us or the driver, a very late order, or a duplicate payment. Give a short description and, where possible, a photo. Our support team reviews each case fairly and tells you the outcome. This can be a full refund, a partial refund for the affected items, or no refund if the claim cannot be supported.

4. If you are not available

If the driver cannot reach you after the waiting period, or you refuse a correct order, you may not be eligible for a refund.

5. How and when you get your money

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Timing depends on your bank and the payment provider, usually 5 to 10 business days. Tips are refunded if the delivery did not happen. We will keep you updated while the refund is “pending”.

6. Contact

Use Help / Support on your order, or email skraaldelights@gmail.com or WhatsApp 067 950 5602 with your order number.
