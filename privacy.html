This policy explains how Skraal Delights handles your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). The responsible party is Skraal Technologies (registration number 2026/610372/07), 3818 Block L4, Kekana Gardens, Hammanskraal, 0400. Our Information Officer is Gontse Kevin Mokopi (Gontsemokopi0@gmail.com).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, surname, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name, surname, email address and profile photo from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Location and addresses: saved delivery addresses and map pins, and your device location when you allow it, to check our delivery area and show nearby stores and delivery progress.
  • Orders: items, store, amounts, fees, tips, delivery or pickup choice, delivery instructions, order status history, ratings and receipts.
  • Payments: the payment status and reference number from our payment provider. We do not store your card number or CVV.
  • Support: messages, complaint details and any photos you send as evidence.
  • Device and app data: push notification token, app version and device type, and basic usage and crash information collected through Firebase.
  • Photos: a profile photo, only if you choose to add one.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and verify your email address.
  • To process orders, payments, deliveries, pickups, refunds and support requests.
  • To send order updates and important service messages, including push notifications.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep records the law requires.
  • To improve the app, using aggregated usage and crash information.

We process your information because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, because you have given consent (for example, for location access), because the law requires it, or because it is in our legitimate interest to keep the service safe.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed:

  • The store you order from: your first name, order details and delivery or pickup instructions.
  • The driver (delivery orders): your name, delivery address, delivery instructions and the contact details needed to complete the delivery. Drivers see this only for their active job.
  • Our payment providers (Ozow and PayFast): to process payments and refunds.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: hosting (Render), database (Neon), email delivery (Brevo), push notifications and analytics (Google Firebase), maps and routing (Mapbox), image storage (Cloudinary) and Google sign-in.
  • Authorities or advisers, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

4. Information leaving South Africa

Some of our service providers store or process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard that is comparable to POPIA.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. Order and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires, normally up to 5 years. Location history and support evidence are kept for 12 months and then deleted or anonymised. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal information, except records we must keep (see Delete your account).

6. Security

We use secure connections, access controls by role, and limit staff access to what their job needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can withdraw your consent at any time (for example by turning off location in your phone settings), but some features may then stop working. To use any of these rights, email skraaldelights@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

8. Children

The app is not for children under 18. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian.

9. Permissions in the app

The app may ask for location (to check delivery coverage and show delivery progress), notifications (order updates), and photos or camera (to add a profile picture or attach evidence to a complaint). You can change these at any time in your phone settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app. The latest version is always available at this address.
