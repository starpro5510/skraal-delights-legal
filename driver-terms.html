1. About these terms

These terms apply between you and Skraal Delights (“Skraal”, “we”, “us”), operated by Skraal Technologies (registration number 2026/610372/07), 3818 Block L4, Kekana Gardens, Hammanskraal, 0400. They govern your use of the Skraal Driver app and your work as a delivery driver on our platform. By signing up, or by going online in the app, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the Driver app.

These terms work together with our Driver Privacy Policy. The customer-facing rules (such as the Terms of Service and the Cancellation & Refund Policy) also affect how deliveries work, so please read them.

2. Who can become a driver

To deliver with Skraal you must:

  • be at least 18 years old;
  • be legally allowed to live and work in South Africa, and have a valid South African ID or passport;
  • hold a valid driver’s licence for the vehicle you use (where a licence is required by law);
  • use a roadworthy, legally registered vehicle (for example a motorbike, scooter, car or bicycle where we allow it); and
  • have a smartphone that can run the Driver app, with a working data connection and GPS.

You must give correct information and keep it up to date. Giving false documents or details is a serious breach of these terms.

3. Your relationship with Skraal

Skraal is a platform that connects customers, stores and drivers. You are an independent service provider, not an employee, partner or agent of Skraal. Nothing in these terms creates an employment relationship.

This means that, within the rules of the platform: you choose when to go online and which deliveries to accept; you use your own vehicle, phone and equipment and carry their costs (fuel, data, maintenance, licences and so on); and you are responsible for your own tax and insurance (see section 10). We may set reasonable rules about how deliveries must be done so that customers and stores are treated fairly and safely.

4. Documents, vehicle and approval

Before you can receive deliveries, you must submit the documents we ask for in the app. These are a selfie, your South African ID or passport, your driver’s licence, and your vehicle details and photos. Our team reviews them, and we may approve or decline an application, or ask for more information, at our discretion.

You must tell us straight away if your licence is suspended, expires or is cancelled, if your vehicle stops being roadworthy, or if anything else you told us stops being true. We may ask you to update or re-submit documents from time to time.

5. Using the Driver app

Your account is personal to you. Do not share your login, let anyone else deliver on your account, or use another person’s account. Keep your password private and tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.

You may not interfere with the app or try to cheat it, for example by faking your location, using automated tools, or going online while you are not able to deliver. If the app has a problem, tell our support team.

6. Accepting and completing deliveries

When a delivery is offered to you, the app shows the pickup store, the drop-off area and your delivery fee. You do not have to accept a delivery. Once you accept, you must complete it properly, or contact support immediately if you genuinely cannot.

You must: go to the store promptly; collect the correct order and check that the order number matches; keep the food secure, upright and as warm or cold as it should be; follow the route and instructions in the app; and deliver to the address shown. Do not open, tamper with, taste or swap any part of an order. Do not collect or deliver orders outside the app.

If you cannot reach the customer, wait for up to 10 minutes at the address and use the contact options in the app. After that, contact support. Do not leave the order with someone else, or dispose of it, unless support tells you to. We decide in each case what happens to the order, and whether you are paid for the trip.

7. Handover and the delivery PIN

At the store, the store confirms the handover to you, which can include scanning your handover code in the app. At the customer, you must collect the customer’s delivery PIN before you complete the delivery. Only hand the order over after you have received the correct PIN from the customer.

Never ask a customer to give you the PIN before you have the order in your hands, and never complete or try to complete a delivery without it. Wrong PIN attempts are limited. If a customer cannot give the PIN, contact support. Attempting to get around the PIN or any other security feature may lead to immediate removal from the platform.

8. Delivery fees, tips and payouts

Delivery fee. The delivery fee you will earn is shown in the app before you accept a delivery. If we later correct a clear mistake, we will tell you.

Tips. Customers may add a tip. 100% of the tip goes to you. Tips are shown separately from your delivery fee. If the delivery did not happen, the tip is refunded to the customer.

Your earnings. Your completed deliveries and earnings are recorded in the app. If an order is refunded because of something you did (for example, food that was lost, damaged, tampered with or delivered to the wrong place), we may reduce or withhold your earnings for that delivery, after hearing your side.

Payouts. You can request a payout of your available earnings to the bank account you registered in the app. The account must be in your own name. Our team reviews payout requests and pays them to your bank. The expected timing is shown in the app. Bank or payment provider delays are outside our control. You must not ask a customer or a store for any extra payment.

9. Taxes and insurance

You are responsible for your own income tax and for any other tax, registration or licence that applies to you as an independent service provider, including declaring your earnings to SARS where required.

You must keep your vehicle insured as the law requires. Skraal does not provide vehicle insurance, personal accident cover or medical cover for you. We recommend that you check that your own insurance covers delivery work.

10. Safety and conduct

Obey all road laws and drive safely. Do not use your phone in your hand while driving or riding, and do not deliver while tired, or under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Wear a helmet and appropriate protective gear where the law or safety requires it.

Treat customers, store staff, other drivers and our team with respect. You may not harass, threaten, discriminate against or abuse anyone, carry weapons while delivering, or ask customers for anything beyond the delivery. Keep your hands, vehicle and delivery bag clean, and keep food separate from anything that could contaminate it.

If there is an accident, an emergency or a safety risk, look after yourself and others first, then tell our support team as soon as you can.

11. Location and data

While you are online and while you are on a delivery, the app shares your precise location with us, including in the background, so that we can match you with orders and show customers where their delivery is. You can stop this by going offline. See the Driver Privacy Policy for what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights.

The app may also ask for “Display over other apps” permission, only to show a small button on top of your navigation app so that you can return to Skraal quickly.

12. Ratings, complaints and support

Customers can rate completed deliveries. We use ratings, complaints and delivery records to keep the service safe and fair. If a customer or store lodges a complaint, we will look at it fairly and give you a chance to respond where we can. Contact support through the app if you have a problem with a delivery, a customer, a store or a payout.

13. Suspension and ending

You can stop delivering and delete your account at any time (see Delete your account). Any earnings you are owed for completed deliveries will still be paid.

We may suspend or close your account, immediately where needed, if you break these terms or the law, give false documents or information, behave in an unsafe or abusive way, commit fraud or theft, put customers or others at risk, or repeatedly fail to complete deliveries properly. Where it is fair and practical, we will tell you the reason and let you respond.

14. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. We are not responsible for losses caused by things outside our reasonable control, such as network failures, load-shedding, road closures or weather.

You are responsible for your own vehicle, equipment, fines, accidents and conduct while delivering. You agree to make good any loss that Skraal, a customer or a store suffers because of your fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence. Nothing in these terms limits any right that cannot lawfully be limited under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 where it applies.

15. Changes

We may update these terms. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. If you keep going online after that, you accept the updated terms. If you do not agree, you can stop delivering and delete your account.

16. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by South African law. If you have a complaint or dispute, please contact us first so that we can try to solve it. If we cannot, either of us may approach a competent South African court or forum.
