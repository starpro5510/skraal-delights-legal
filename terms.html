1. About these terms

These terms apply when you use the Skraal Delights customer app and related services. By creating an account or placing an order you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Who can use the app

You must be 18 or older, or have the permission of a parent or guardian. You must give correct information and keep it up to date. You are responsible for everything done through your account.

3. What Skraal does

Skraal is a platform that connects customers with independent stores and with delivery drivers. The stores prepare the food. Drivers deliver it. We do not prepare food ourselves, and we do not guarantee that any store will be open or able to accept your order. Each order is for one store only. If you add items from a second store, you will be asked to finish or clear your current cart first.

4. Creating an account

You can create an account with your email address and a password, or continue with Google. To protect your account, we verify your email address with a one-time code before your account is created. If you use Google, we use your Google name, surname, email address and profile photo to fill in your details. You will still need to add your phone number and a password.

5. Prices, fees and totals

Item prices are set by each store. Before you pay, the app shows an itemised total that can include the food subtotal, a delivery fee (delivery orders only), a service fee, any discount, and an optional tip. Totals are calculated on our servers at checkout, and the amount you see at payment is the amount charged. Prices include VAT where it applies. Pickup orders have no delivery fee.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by Ozow and PayFast. We never see or store your full card number or CVV. Your order is only confirmed once the payment provider confirms that your payment was successful. A success screen alone is not proof of payment. If a payment is pending or unclear, we check the result with the provider before asking you to pay again, so you are not charged twice. Only the payment methods shown at checkout are supported.

7. Delivery

We deliver within Hammanskraal and surrounding areas. Delivery times are estimates and can change because of how busy the store is, driver availability, traffic or weather. We will tell you honestly if finding a driver is taking longer than usual.

You must be reachable and available at the delivery address. If you are not, the driver will try to contact you and wait for up to 10 minutes. After that, our support team will decide what happens next, and the order may be closed without a refund if the food cannot be delivered because of you.

8. Delivery PIN

For delivery orders, you will receive a PIN in the app. Give the PIN to the driver only when you have your order in your hands. Never share it earlier or with anyone else. The driver cannot complete the delivery without it, and wrong attempts are limited. If you cannot give the PIN, contact support.

9. Pickup orders

When the store marks your order as ready, we will notify you. Show your order number to the store when you collect. Orders that are not collected within 60 minutes may be cancelled without a refund, after we have reminded you.

10. Cancellations and refunds

Whether you can cancel, and what you get back, depends on the stage of your order. The full rules are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy and form part of these terms.

11. Allergies and food safety

Stores are responsible for the food they prepare, its ingredients and its safety. If you have an allergy or dietary need, tell the store in your order notes and contact them before ordering. We cannot guarantee that any item is free of allergens. Please check your order before accepting it. Do not eat food you believe is unsafe, and report it to support straight away with a photo.

12. Tips

You can add an optional tip for the driver at checkout or after delivery (10%, 20%, 30% or 40% of the food subtotal). Tips are shown separately from fees. 100% of the tip goes to the driver.

13. Ratings and reviews

You can rate completed orders only, and once per order. Reviews must be honest and respectful. We may remove reviews that are abusive, false or unrelated to the order.

14. Acceptable use

You may not misuse the app, give false information, use someone else’s account or payment method, abuse refund or complaint processes, harass stores or drivers, try to bypass the delivery PIN or any security feature, or interfere with the service. We may suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

15. Your account and security

Keep your password and delivery PIN private. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account. You can delete your account at any time (see Delete your account).
