This policy explains how Skraal Technologies handles the personal information of store managers and owners who use the Skraal Store Management app, under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). The responsible party is Skraal Technologies (registration number 2026/610372/07), 3818 Block L4, Kekana Gardens, Hammanskraal, 0400. Our Information Officer is Gontse Kevin Mokopi (Gontsemokopi0@gmail.com).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, surname, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never in plain text.
  • Store details: store name, description, address, map location, phone number, category, photos, opening hours and delivery area.
  • Menu and promotions: product names, descriptions, the in-store prices you upload, photos and promo codes you create.
  • Orders: the items, status, delivery or pickup choice, notes, and the customer’s first name and the order details needed to prepare and hand over each order.
  • Banking details: bank name, account holder, account number and branch code, so we can pay your store’s earnings.
  • Sales and payout records: order totals, commission, payout requests and payments, and customer ratings of your store.
  • Support: messages and reports you send to us.
  • Device and app data: your push notification token, app version and basic crash information collected through Firebase.

2. Camera and photos

The app uses your camera in two situations, and only when you choose to: to scan the driver’s handover code when an order is collected, and to take or choose photos of your store and products. Photos you add are stored with our image storage provider and shown to customers.

3. Why we use it

  • To set up and run your store on Skraal, and to check and approve it.
  • To show your store, menu and prices to customers.
  • To send you new orders and order updates, including push notifications.
  • To calculate commission and sales, and to pay you.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, handle complaints and support requests, and keep records the law requires.
  • To improve the app, using aggregated usage and crash information.

We process your information because it is needed to work with you as a store partner, because you have given consent (for example, for notifications or the camera), because the law requires it, or because it is in our legitimate interest to keep the service safe.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed:

  • Customers: your store name, address, location, photos, menu, prices (shown with Skraal’s mark-up once it applies, see the Store Partner Terms), opening hours and rating.
  • Drivers: your store name, address, location and the details of orders they collect, including handover verification.
  • Our team: administrators who approve stores, review payouts and handle support. Your banking details are used only to pay you.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: hosting (Render), database (Neon), email delivery (Brevo), push notifications (Google Firebase) and image storage (Cloudinary).
  • Authorities or advisers, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

5. Information leaving South Africa

Some of our service providers store or process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard that is comparable to POPIA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and store information while your store account is active. When your account is closed we delete your personal details, banking details and store photos, and anonymise your profile. Order, sales, commission and payout records are kept for as long as the law requires, normally up to 5 years, without your personal details.

7. Security

We use secure connections, access controls by role, and limit staff access to what their job needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and do not share your login.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can withdraw your consent at any time (for example by turning off notifications or camera access in your phone settings), but some features may then stop working. To close your account or use any other right, email skraaldelights@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or see Delete your account. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

9. Who can use the app

The Skraal Store Management app is for business use by people who are 18 or older.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app. The latest version is always available at this address.
