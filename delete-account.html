Delete Your Account & Data

Skraal Delights customer app and Skraal Driver appLast updated 25 October 2026

  2. Without the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. Before you delete

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data in either of the ways below. This applies to the Skraal Delights customer app and the Skraal Driver app, both run by Skraal Technologies.

In the app

  • Customer app: open Settings → Delete Account and confirm with your password.
  • Driver app: open Settings → Delete account and confirm with your password.

Your account is closed straight away.

Without the app

Email skraaldelights@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. Please say whether it is a customer or driver account, and include your name and phone number so we can verify you. We confirm your request and complete it within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Customers: name, surname, email, phone number, saved addresses, profile photo, notification token, favourites and login details. Your order history is detached from your identity.
  • Drivers: name, surname, email, phone number, selfie, ID and licence documents, vehicle details, bank details, location and login details.

Before you delete

  • Active orders (customers) or active deliveries (drivers) must be completed or cancelled first.
  • Drivers must withdraw their earnings first. If you still have unpaid earnings, request your payout, or contact us.

What we must keep

We keep records the law or a dispute requires, such as payment and invoice records, delivery, earnings and payout records, refund and chargeback records and fraud-prevention logs, for 5 years. These no longer contain your personal details, are kept securely, and are used only for those purposes.

Other data requests

To see a copy of your information, correct it, or object to how it is used, email skraaldelights@gmail.com. The Information Officer is Gontse Kevin Mokopi (Gontsemokopi0@gmail.com).
