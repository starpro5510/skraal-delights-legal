1. About these terms

These terms apply between you (the “Store”) and Skraal Delights (“Skraal”, “we”, “us”), operated by Skraal Technologies (registration number 2026/610372/07), 3818 Block L4, Kekana Gardens, Hammanskraal, 0400. They govern your use of the Skraal Store Management app and the sale of your food and products through the Skraal platform. By registering a store, or by using the app, you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

These terms work together with our Store Privacy Policy, the customer Terms of Service and the Cancellation & Refund Policy.

2. Who can partner with us

To sell on Skraal you must:

  • be a registered business, or a person with the legal right to trade and to sign for the store;
  • operate from premises in or near our delivery area, and hold every licence, permit and certificate the law requires for preparing and selling food from those premises (including any municipal health and food-handling approvals); and
  • give us correct, up-to-date information about you and your store.

The person who registers the store confirms that they are authorised to accept these terms on the store’s behalf.

3. Your relationship with Skraal

Skraal is a platform that connects customers, stores and drivers. You are the seller of your food and products. You prepare the food, you are responsible for its quality, safety and ingredients, and you are an independent business, not an employee, partner or agent of Skraal. Drivers are independent service providers, not employees of the store or of Skraal.

4. Store account and approval

You register your store in the Store Management app with your details, store information, location, photos, opening hours and banking details. Our team reviews each store and may approve, decline, or ask for more information at our discretion. A store can only receive orders after it has been approved.

Keep your login private and do not share it with people who should not have access. You are responsible for everything done through your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.

5. Menu and prices

You create and maintain your own menu in the app: item names, descriptions, photos, options and prices. You must make sure your menu is accurate, current and complete, including ingredients and allergen information you give in the item description.

The price you upload must be your normal in-store price for that item at the time. When you change your in-store price, you must update it in the app. Do not upload prices that are higher than the prices you charge customers in your store, and do not add your own extra mark-up for the Skraal platform. If an item is out of stock, mark it unavailable straight away.

6. Launch pricing and the 10% mark-up

We want stores to get established on Skraal without extra pricing pressure. For that reason:

At launch (the first 3 months). When your store is approved and goes live on Skraal (your “go-live date”), you upload your in-store prices and the platform shows them to customers exactly as you uploaded them. We call the first 3 months after your go-live date the “Launch Period”. No mark-up is added to your item prices during the Launch Period.

After 3 months (the 10% mark-up). From the day after your Launch Period ends, Skraal adds a 10% mark-up to every item on your menu. This includes items you add later. The mark-up is added on top of the price you upload, so customers see and pay your in-store price plus 10%. You keep uploading your normal in-store price. The mark-up is applied by the platform automatically. The mark-up is shown to customers as part of the item price in the app and does not change the price you uploaded.

Example itemPrice you upload (in-store price)Customer pays in the app
During the Launch Period (first 3 months)R100.00R100.00
After the Launch Period (10% mark-up)R100.00R110.00

The marked-up price is rounded to the nearest cent. The mark-up applies to the price of items. It does not apply to delivery fees, service fees or tips, which are shown separately at checkout.

We will tell you in the app before the mark-up begins for your store, and the Store Management app will show the Launch Period end date. Our customer Terms of Service tell customers that prices in the app can be higher than in-store prices after a store’s first 3 months. If we change the mark-up percentage or the length of the Launch Period in future, we will give you notice in the app before the change takes effect, and the change will not apply to orders already placed.

7. Orders

New orders appear in the app and we notify you. You must respond quickly. If you do not accept or reject an order within 5 minutes, the order may be cancelled and the customer refunded in full. You should only accept orders that you can prepare, and you should give an honest preparation time.

Prepare the order exactly as the customer ordered it, including any notes about options or allergies. Update the order status in the app as it moves from accepted, to preparing, to ready. Do not cancel accepted orders without a good reason. Repeated rejected, late or cancelled orders may affect your standing on the platform.

8. Food safety, allergens and packaging

You are responsible for the food you prepare and sell. You must prepare, store and handle food in line with the law and good hygiene practice, use safe and clean packaging that will survive delivery, and seal the order so that it cannot be tampered with.

If a customer tells you about an allergy or dietary need, you must take it seriously and tell the customer if you cannot safely meet it. Do not claim an item is free of an allergen unless you are sure. Do not give an order to a driver if you believe the food is unsafe.

9. Handover to drivers and pickup customers

For delivery orders, when the driver arrives, check that the order number matches and hand over the order. The app may ask you to confirm the handover, which can include scanning the driver’s handover code with your camera. Do not hand an order to someone who cannot show the correct order number or handover code.

For pickup orders, mark the order ready in the app, and give it to the customer when they show the order number. Orders not collected within 60 minutes may be cancelled without a refund, after we have reminded the customer.

10. Commission, earnings and payouts

Your earnings. You earn the price you uploaded for the items on completed orders, less the commission or platform fee that applies to your store (if any), as shown in the Store Management app. Delivery fees belong to the driver. Tips belong to the driver. The 10% mark-up described in section 6 is a platform charge to the customer and is not part of your uploaded price.

Payouts. You can request a payout of your available earnings to the bank account you registered. The account must belong to the store or to you. Our team reviews payout requests and pays them to your bank. The expected timing is shown in the app. We may delay a payout while we look into suspected fraud, a dispute or a refund.

Taxes. You are responsible for your own taxes, including income tax and VAT if you are registered for VAT. Item prices include VAT where it applies.

The commission and payout details applying to your store are shown in the app, and we will give you notice in the app before changing them.

11. Cancellations, refunds and problems

The rules for cancellations and refunds are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy and apply to your orders. In short: if you reject or cannot complete an order, the customer is refunded in full. If a customer reports a problem with a delivered order (missing or wrong items, quality or safety), our support team reviews it fairly and may ask you for your side or for photos.

If a refund is approved because of your mistake, such as wrong or missing items, unsafe food or poor quality, we may deduct the refunded amount (or the affected part of it) from your earnings. Where a customer cancels in line with the refund policy before you have prepared the food, you will not be paid for that order.

12. Your content and photos

You keep ownership of your store name, logos, photos and menu content. You give Skraal a free, non-exclusive right to use, show and store that content in the app and on our platform and marketing so that customers can see and order from your store. You confirm that you own, or have the right to use, everything you upload and that it does not break anyone else’s rights. We may remove content that is false, misleading, offensive or unlawful.

13. Conduct

You must treat customers, drivers and our team respectfully and honestly. You may not give false information, manipulate ratings or reviews, create fake orders, misuse promotions, or try to get around the platform’s security, handover or payment steps. You may not use customer or driver information for any purpose other than fulfilling the order.

14. Data protection

When you receive customer details, such as a customer’s first name and order details, you must only use them to prepare and hand over that order, and keep them safe. See the Store Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.

15. Suspension and ending

You can stop selling on Skraal, or ask us to close your store account, at any time (see Delete your account). Completed orders will still be settled and paid out.

We may suspend or close your store immediately where needed if you break these terms or the law, sell unsafe food, give false information, commit fraud, mistreat customers or drivers, or repeatedly fail to handle orders properly. Where it is fair and practical, we will tell you the reason and let you respond.

16. Our responsibility

We work hard to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error-free. We are not responsible for losses caused by things outside our reasonable control, such as network failures, load-shedding or payment provider downtime. We are not responsible for the preparation, ingredients or safety of the food you sell, and you agree to make good any loss that Skraal or a customer suffers because of your fraud, wilful misconduct or negligence. Nothing in these terms limits any right that cannot lawfully be limited under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 where it applies.

17. Changes

We may update these terms. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. If you keep using the app after that, you accept the updated terms. If you do not agree, you can stop selling on Skraal and close your account.

18. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by South African law. If you have a complaint or dispute, please contact us first so that we can try to solve it. If we cannot, either of us may approach a competent South African court or forum.
