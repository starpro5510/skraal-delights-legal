This policy explains how Skraal Technologies handles the personal information of delivery drivers who use the Skraal Driver app, under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). The responsible party is Skraal Technologies (registration number 2026/610372/07), 3818 Block L4, Kekana Gardens, Hammanskraal, 0400. Our Information Officer is Gontse Kevin Mokopi (Gontsemokopi0@gmail.com).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, surname, email address, phone number, age, gender and password. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never in plain text.
  • Verification documents: a selfie, your South African ID or passport, your driver’s licence, and photos or details of your vehicle. These are used to check that you are allowed to deliver.
  • Vehicle details: vehicle type, registration number, model and colour.
  • Banking details: bank name, account holder, account number and branch code, so we can pay your earnings.
  • Location: your precise location while you are online and during deliveries (see section 3).
  • Delivery and earnings records: deliveries you accept, complete or reject, delivery fees, payout requests and payments, and customer ratings of your deliveries.
  • Support: messages and reports you send to us.
  • Device and app data: basic app and crash information, and the settings you choose in the app.

2. Why we use it

  • To check your identity and eligibility, and to keep customers and other drivers safe.
  • To offer you deliveries, track them and show customers and stores their progress.
  • To calculate your earnings and pay you.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, handle complaints and support requests, and keep records the law requires.
  • To improve the app, using aggregated usage and crash information.

We process your information because it is needed to work with you as a driver, because you have given consent (for example, for location access), because the law requires it, or because it is in our legitimate interest to keep the service safe.

3. Location, including in the background

While you are online and while you are on a delivery, the app shares your precise location with us, even when the app is in the background or your screen is off. Android shows a notification while this is happening. We use it to match you with nearby orders, show customers where their delivery is, work out routes and arrival times, and settle disputes about a delivery. The app does not share your location when you are offline. You can stop at any time by going offline, or by turning off location in your phone settings, but you will not be able to receive deliveries.

4. Display over other apps

The app asks for “Display over other apps” only to show a small floating button on top of your navigation app (such as Google Maps or Waze), so you can return to Skraal quickly. This permission does not collect any information.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed:

  • Customers: your first name, vehicle details and live location while you are delivering their order.
  • Stores: your first name and vehicle details when you collect an order.
  • Our team: administrators who check your documents, review payouts and handle support. Your bank details are used only to pay you.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: hosting (Render), database (Neon), email delivery (Brevo), maps and routing (Mapbox), and image storage (Cloudinary).
  • Authorities or advisers, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

6. Information leaving South Africa

Some of our service providers store or process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard that is comparable to POPIA.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information and verification documents while your driver account is active. When you delete your account we delete your documents, selfie, vehicle details and bank details, and anonymise your profile. Delivery, earnings and payout records are kept for as long as the law requires, normally up to 5 years, without your personal details. Location data is kept for up to 12 months.

8. Security

Your documents are stored privately and only you and our administrators can open them. We use secure connections and limit staff access to what their job needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can withdraw your consent at any time (for example by going offline or turning off location), but some features may then stop working. You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or see Delete your account. To use any other right, email skraaldelights@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

10. Age

The Skraal Driver app is only for people who are 18 or older.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will tell you in the app. The latest version is always available at this address.
